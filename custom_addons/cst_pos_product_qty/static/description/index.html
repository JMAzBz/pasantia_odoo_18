POS: Display Product Quantity on Product Cards
This module enhances the Point of Sale interface by showing the On Hand quantity for each product directly on the POS product card. It helps POS users quickly view stock levels while processing orders, improving efficiency and reducing stock-related errors.

Detailed Features List

Show Product Quantity on POS Product Cards

  • The module displays the On Hand product quantity directly on each product card in the POS interface.
  • This helps cashiers instantly verify whether an item is in stock during order processing.

Real time Quantity Update on Product Screen

  • The POS automatically displays the latest available stock for each product on its product card.
  • This ensures that cashiers always see accurate and updated quantities while processing sales, improving decision-making and reducing stock-related errors.
